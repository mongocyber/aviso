# Aviso 20s HyperFrames Design

## Style Prompt

Modern privacy-first browser product promo. The video should feel like a calm but capable productivity tool, not a speculative AI launch. Use grounded browser UI, clear Chinese copy, and restrained motion that keeps the product value readable in two seconds.

## Colors

- Background: `#111714` deep green-charcoal.
- Foreground: `#F4F1EA` warm off-white.
- Secondary text: `#CBD3C5` muted sage-gray.
- Brand accent: `#6F8C73` Aviso sage.
- Warm emphasis: `#C79A5B` restrained amber.
- UI ink: `#202823` dark panel ink.

## Typography

- Primary Chinese UI text: `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, `Microsoft YaHei`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
- Headlines use heavy weight and large sizes for video readability.
- Body copy stays above 28px; small labels stay above 18px.

## What Not To Do

- Do not claim Chrome Web Store approval or public release.
- Do not mention free credits, subscription, hosted Aviso AI, login, telemetry, analytics, web search, or project-owned backend.
- Do not make screenshot asking a main selling point.
- Do not use sci-fi neon, purple-blue gradients, or generic AI orb visuals.
- Do not invent provider status beyond BYOK direct-provider configuration.
